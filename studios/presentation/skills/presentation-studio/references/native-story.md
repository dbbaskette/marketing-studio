# The native page's slide story

New researched runner desks use one bounded Quick-profile scan, then wait at **Review the slide story** before detailed research. This does not add a nested assistant or extra work to focused conversation commands.

Edit section titles, messages and one to three research questions per section. Add or remove sections; keep or revise separately listed questions from the form. **Save story** saves locally without model work. **Approve story & research** binds the exact story and intake/source choices, then researches only the retained scope. A changed input or concurrent worker prevents an outdated decision.

The scan uses bounded supplied excerpts, no external searches, and at most 180 seconds and $0.25 within the deck's existing limits. Stop or timeout keeps accepted output or a usable incomplete starting story. There is no automatic second author or escalation. The scan supplies no teaching or presentation evidence; Harness Research's independent check still validates the factual research.

The approved story and checked research feed the slide-text draft. Slide-text approval, look choice and delivery remain the person's decisions. A finished script or selected change keeps its direct route, without an unnecessary scan or research pass. Existing records keep their saved flow; schema-5 stories and new schema-6 section flows require their compatible saved runtime.


New section flows keep completed research contributions as drafts, retry only
unfinished contributions and run the supported independent check on the assembled
dossier. A refresh first previews changed inputs and affected sections. Retain
unaffected evidence through the supported protocol; never carry approval merely
because a slide image is unchanged. Notes-only and focused word edits keep their
direct route. Research, local saving and final delivery remain distinct decisions.
