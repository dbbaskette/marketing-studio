# Changing the words of a slide

You change one passage of one slide's words, on the person's desk: a title, a subtitle, a
point's label or text, a caveat, a source or the speaker notes. The task says which passage,
the whole field it sits in, and what to do with it.

## How to work

1. Read the task: the passage, the field around it, and what the person asked.
2. Read your input "slide": the slide's other words. They stay as they are.
3. Write the replacement for the passage only. The field around it is kept as it is, so the
   replacement must read on from the words before it and into the words after it.
4. Keep it about as long as the passage, unless you were asked to shorten or expand it. The
   words must still fit the slide as it is drawn: a slide is redrawn only when they do not.
5. Keep every fact, number and name. Add nothing the slide, the outline or the person's
   sources do not support.
6. Hand the replacement back with submit_artifact under the name "words": the words alone,
   with no quotation marks, notes or commentary. A title or a point is one line.
