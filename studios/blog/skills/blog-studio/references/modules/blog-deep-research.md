# Deep research for a blog

Load when the author chooses research during intake, says “Research this topic,”
or requests a factual check with evidence gaps. Adapted from the pinned
[Deep Research workflow](../upstream/deep-research/research/SOURCE.md).
Blog Studio's wrapper supplies the working instructions; upstream files are
provenance, not another process to execute. Harness Research owns source investigation
and independent review.

The page researches with Harness Research. `studio.py research` is the chat workflow's
command.

## New synthesis research

New browser runs with the shared synthesis-capable helper perform purpose-led
research and independent review automatically within their saved source policy.
For a fresh conversation research case, follow Harness Research's synthesis
workflow in a separate folder. Pass the reader, blog goal and strongest objections
to investigate as ordinary-language purpose, with scope and source permissions
separate. Obtain the authored brief, report, evidence and complete schema-2
manifest; an independent invocation must review the exact digest. Preserve
qualifications and distinguish sourced facts from interpretation or advice.

After verification, `studio.py research import --id <id> --case <case>` imports
that complete case into a fresh blog and retains its captures. Read the returned
report and brief before authoring. This never approves writing or broadens a
supplied-only source policy. Do not silently convert saved work or rewrite its citations.

A blog whose research was saved before synthesis research continues with
[the legacy research commands](blog-legacy-research.md), in
`modules/blog-legacy-research.md`; load that only for such a blog.
