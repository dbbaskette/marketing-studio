# Content strategy module

Keep private inputs in the user's existing harness and selected private hub.
Apply [data boundaries](../privacy.md) before research, media, or document
posting; upstream suggestions do not authorize external disclosure.

Use for topic selection, content pillars, clusters, prioritization, and editorial
planning. For a single article with an agreed brief, use [blog-write](blog-write.md).

**Input:** audience, content goal, brand/product context when relevant, existing
content, customer research, keyword data, and resource constraints already supplied.
**Output:** a prioritized plan with rationale, intended reader/search intent,
content type, and links or clusters where useful; an article brief if requested.

Read the [original content-strategy skill](../upstream/marketingskills/skills/content-strategy/SOURCE.md)
for the planning framework. Open supporting references only for that operation:

- [Content distribution](../upstream/marketingskills/skills/content-strategy/references/content-distribution.md) for distribution plans.
- [Headless CMS](../upstream/marketingskills/skills/content-strategy/references/headless-cms.md) for an actual CMS or editorial architecture decision.

Adapt business and conversion framing for personal, technical, or editorial blogs.
Use provided research before asking questions. Treat numeric prioritization weights,
calendar ratios, and production quotas as starting points. Mark estimated demand
and research gaps; do not invent keyword metrics or customer quotations.
Distribution plans remain plans until the user authorizes external actions.
