# Copywriting module

Use for blog titles, hooks, benefit framing, calls to action, or supporting
marketing copy. For the full researched article use [blog-write](blog-write.md).

**Input:** the target passage or copy task, audience, desired reader action,
voice, offer/product facts, and available evidence.
**Output:** focused copy in the requested format, with headline or CTA
alternatives and concise rationale when those help a decision.

Read the [original copywriting skill](../upstream/marketingskills/skills/copywriting/SOURCE.md)
for persuasion and clarity principles. Open:

- [Copy frameworks](../upstream/marketingskills/skills/copywriting/references/copy-frameworks.md) for headline, value proposition, or page structure work.
- [Natural transitions](../upstream/marketingskills/skills/copywriting/references/natural-transitions.md) for a specific flow problem.

Keep marketing page structures conditional on a marketing deliverable. Preserve
editorial purpose and author voice for blog passages. Numeric benefit examples,
conversion claims, guarantees, and customer proof in the source are examples or
claims to verify; they are not facts about the user's product. Do not add an
offer or conversion step the user did not request. Use [copy-editing](copy-editing.md)
for revision of existing copy rather than automatically rewriting it.
