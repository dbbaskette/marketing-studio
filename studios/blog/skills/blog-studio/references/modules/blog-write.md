# Article writing guide

Keep private inputs in the existing harness and selected private hub. Apply
[data boundaries](../privacy.md) before external research, media, or document
posting; upstream suggestions do not authorize disclosure.

Use this guide for a complete article from a brief or outline. Inputs are the
reader, intended takeaway, chosen voice, selected evidence, and requested format.
Return the draft and only the material unresolved needs. Routine writing needs
this guide, not the archived source.

## Establish the argument

Reuse the saved brief and supplied structure. Ask only for gaps that prevent an
accurate draft: reader, purpose, stance, or essential facts. Do not turn a clear
request into a new intake interview. An article for practitioners can assume
their working vocabulary; an introduction must establish the necessary context.

Identify what the reader should understand or do afterward. Build an internal
outline whose sections each advance that takeaway. Each section needs a distinct
job, relevant support, and a transition from the preceding point. Merge overlapping
sections; introduce each example once and develop it only when its role changes.
An author-supplied outline retains its headings/order unless restructuring is
requested. If it has a material gap, identify it without inventing the missing
position. First-draft mode may outline and continue; outline-only stops before
composition and uses [argument outline](blog-argument-outline.md).

Choose form from the task: a procedure needs ordered steps and prerequisites;
a comparison needs consistent criteria; an argument needs reasons and objections;
a case study needs actual context, action, and results. A generic question-shaped
title, summary box, FAQ, or marketing CTA is not compulsory.

## Build from evidence

Use selected sources according to their roles. Inspiration suggests an angle;
biography establishes background; confirmed authored samples inform voice.
None automatically proves a factual claim. Keep private source names/passages out
of public queries. Supplied-only work stays within supplied evidence.

For a material claim, inspect the supporting passage and retain its source ID or
verified URL. Keep population, period, units, method, uncertainty, and causal limits
intact. Place reader-facing citations near the supported assertion in the chosen
publication style. Use exact quotes only when available; paraphrase faithfully.
A summary or linked title is not proof that the underlying passage was read.

Separate source findings from your interpretation. Explain how the evidence leads
to the recommendation rather than stacking disconnected quotations. Reconcile
conflicting sources or state the disagreement. Mark missing support in editorial
notes; remove unsupported numbers or keep the claim qualitative only if that
qualitative claim is itself supported. Never manufacture testimonials, statistics,
first-hand experience, customer results, or a more confident stance for the author.

## Compose a continuous article

Open with a useful observation, concrete situation, central answer, or supported
finding. Establish why this reader should care, then advance into the first section
instead of restating the introduction. A hypothetical example must be recognizable
as hypothetical; do not write it as the author's lived event.

Give important sections a clear point early, then explanation, evidence, and the
practical implication the reader needs. Define unfamiliar terms where introduced.
Use accurate descriptive headings and a coherent hierarchy. Make claims intelligible
in their context without repeating the thesis at every section boundary.

Apply the selected voice guide and explicit author rules: vocabulary, point of
view, rhythm, degree of formality, and tolerated promotional language. Preserve
technical precision and meaningful qualifiers. Vary pacing to fit the reasoning;
sentence lengths, keywords, statistics, links, media, and section sizes have no
universal quotas. End when the takeaway is earned; add a next action only when it
serves the article's purpose and the user's request.

Use real verified links with descriptive anchors. Keep unresolved link suggestions
outside finished prose. Add summaries, visuals, tables, or FAQs when they clarify
the material; optional assets do not block the requested draft. Never send private
drafts/titles to image or analysis providers merely because those tools exist.

## Deliver and preserve

Read the complete draft for argument flow, repeated examples, unsupported promises,
voice drift, and accidental changes to names, numbers, quotes, or links. Fix relevant
problems within the authorized task. Deep review, SEO scoring, humanization, export,
and repurposing are separate requested operations, not mandatory delivery gates.
Neither a style check nor a GEO score establishes authorship or future rankings.

Follow the [workspace router](../workspace.md) and article operation to save the
draft/checkpoint while preserving original/history and exact dependency pins.
Deliver the requested artifact, with concise evidence or asset gaps when needed.
Do not add promotional footers or claim omitted preflight/reviewer tools ran.
