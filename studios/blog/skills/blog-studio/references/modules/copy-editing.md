# Copy editing module

Keep private inputs in the user's existing harness and selected private hub.
Apply [data boundaries](../privacy.md) before research, media, or document
posting; upstream suggestions do not authorize external disclosure.

Use for polishing, proofreading, editorial review, or refreshing existing blog
content while preserving its central message and voice.

**Input:** existing draft, intended audience, voice requirements, known concerns,
source material, and whether the user wants edits, comments, or a rewrite.
**Output:** the requested revised draft or specific editorial feedback, plus
material change notes and any unresolved factual checks.

Read the [original copy-editing skill](../upstream/marketingskills/skills/copy-editing/SOURCE.md)
for clarity, voice, relevance, proof, specificity, and focused editing passes.
Load only the relevant support:

- [Editing checklist](../upstream/marketingskills/skills/copy-editing/references/checklist.md) for a comprehensive review.
- [Plain English alternatives](../upstream/marketingskills/skills/copy-editing/references/plain-english-alternatives.md) for word-level simplification.
- [Content refresh](../upstream/marketingskills/skills/copy-editing/references/content-refresh.md) for stale material or a refresh-versus-rewrite decision.

Scale review depth to the request. Conversion, emotional, and risk-reversal
passes apply only where the passage has a relevant persuasive goal. Preserve
technical precision; sentence limits and reading-level guidance are heuristics.
Use verified evidence for specificity instead of inventing numbers or
promises. An expert persona exercise is optional and must not be presented as
independent human or agent review. Apply edits directly when already authorized;
do not require the author to resubmit copy after every sweep. Report meaningful
changes and remaining gaps once the requested revision is complete.
