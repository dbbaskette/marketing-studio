# Structure feedback

For feedback on argument and organization, read the selected draft, brief and
voice. Identify each section's contribution to the central point. Locate missing
reasoning, repeated setups, abrupt transitions, misplaced support, or unanswered
reader objections. Full-article structure needs the full draft; label passage-only
feedback accordingly.

For software-product posts, reuse the blog type and reader purpose from the brief.
When assessing the form, load only the selected row of [article forms](../style/forms.md).
Judge whether the article fulfills its promise, not template-heading compliance.
Do not re-run intake or turn a shape check into unrequested code/benchmark execution.

Quote the target, explain the reader's difficulty, and propose a specific move,
cut, transition, or missing example. Request unknown facts rather than inventing
an anecdote. Preserve the author's stance, scope and deliberate organization.
Flag claims for evidence review; structure alone cannot verify their truth.

Save requested feedback as `shape` with scope and findings through
[review storage](../workspace/reviews.md). Do not replace the manuscript for a
feedback-only request. Load [editing](../modules/copy-editing.md) only when asked
to perform the revision. Keep private drafts in this harness and selected hub.
