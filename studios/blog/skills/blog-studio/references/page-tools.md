# Contextual page tools

In a manuscript, choose **Tools**. From Blogs, use **Library and team tools
→ Tools** for global tasks. Normal writing and polishing require none of
these options. Opening a tool starts no model, research or Google write.

- **Voices and styles:** a voice is one author's; a style is a kind of writing. Open it from Home (Set up before you write), the sidebar's Voices, Settings or Tools.
  Add samples by upload, links, a LinkedIn export or an existing source, learn a
  provisional guide, edit, audition, explicitly confirm, export or retire.
  Choosing a revision for an article is separate from changing defaults. Existing
  articles keep their pins. Sparse samples do not establish biography or expertise.
- **Interview me:** record, revise or skip answers; pause and resume from Blogs.
  Ask for one adaptive question or prepare an outline when enough substance exists.
  Read/edit the outline before explicitly starting the normal draft flow on that
  article. Answers are author-reported; missing evidence stays missing.
- **Saved manuscript revisions:** compare a text checkpoint, then explicitly
  restore locally. Save/copy unsaved text first. Google and source/voice pins stay
  unchanged; the prior manuscript remains recoverable.
- **Compare Google and local text:** inspect accepted Google text, the common
  baseline and local edits. Resolve overlapping passages, then accept locally.
  It rechecks the Doc before saving, keeps pending Google review and sends nothing.
  Finish or clear local wording proposals before changing their baseline.
- **Repurpose this blog:** choose one channel and target length. Review/edit the
  result and save a named copy separately. Counts are actual editorial counts,
  not verified platform limits. Nothing posts or schedules. Missing URLs stay
  marked. Saved copies and interrupted/failed tasks reopen from Tools.
- **Clarity and citation readiness / title and opening:** prepare located
  suggestions in the existing editor. Choose one alternative per passage, adjust
  it, and explicitly save selected edits. No invented evidence or automatic CTA.
- **Open as a Google Doc:** on Done, confirm a private Doc of the approved draft.
  A verified readback links it; an uncertain upload is reconciled rather than repeated.
  Pre-publish and Repurpose are on Done too. Sharing stays separate.
- **Pre-publish:** local report only: headings, placeholders, link syntax, image
  alternatives and selected evidence IDs. No factual, rights or live-link claim.
- **Check topic overlap:** bounded local reference leads; historical posts require
  current verification. No readiness, calendar or publication decision changes.

Generated tasks use the existing single-worker queue and saved assistant/model
choices. Cancel stops the selected task; retries are explicit and bounded. Results
wait for review; changed source inputs prevent applying older output. Submitted
answers/results persist on this computer; unfinished browser text is retained
while navigating tools but is not a cross-computer transcript or backup.

**New blog from my files** on Home takes each upload with its role, facts, approved
messaging or an example of the kind of post (changeable per file until Start), asks
whether to find three angles or start from the point the person knows, and chooses the
voice with **Write as**, the shape with **Kind of post** and the depth with **Length**.
The Upload dialog's **Use this file as** offers the same roles. A draft's gate lists
what the writing left for the author under **Needs you before publishing**.

**The team library** (Blogs → Library and team tools → Team library) holds every
finished blog, kept file, link and Google Doc, each with a role, author and kind of
post. **Add to library** takes files, a list of links and a Doc in one sheet with one
role, author and kind; **Each post's own author** imports first, reads each page's
byline, then groups the posts by author to rename, merge, complete, mark as voice
samples (on by default) and learn a voice per group, with the usual confirmation.
Filters for role, kind and author; a selection learns a style or a voice from the
items or starts a blog with them as examples. **From the library** on the files card
picks items with their library role, changeable for that blog only; a learned kind
of post pre-ticks its samples. **Settings → Default voice** names a confirmed voice
for every new blog, and **Interview me** takes **Write as** too.

**A run that stops** says what to do beside the error: **Open Status** and **What the
assistant said** for a CLI failure, where the step time limit lives beside **Try again
with more time**, and, at research, **What the research check objected to**, what the
last attempt took and **Continue without research**. First-run setup opens **Writing
preferences** and marks an assistant that is not installed.

Conversation methods remain available through the normal operation references.
The report commands are `studio.py --root WORKSPACE prepublish --id ID` and
`studio.py --root WORKSPACE plan --topic TOPIC`; neither changes a manuscript.

## Research review

The research stage shows the brief, independent check's note, gaps, source limitations
and question mapping, with report and evidence in folds. Ask for changes shows the full
research rerun estimate when research is selected, and updates it if another stage is
chosen. New blogs researching your documents only instead show Claims in your material, an
exact-source map with one correction; from-files keeps its existing flow. A review says how
many pages were outside your sites. Settings supplies Home's research default (your
documents, your sites, the public web) for new writing; Explore ideas, Revise and Review use none.

The Done card also opens pre-publish checks and repurposing directly. **Apply the review's findings** at review saves the prior draft and confirms the three jobs before restarting draft, review and handoff. The complete saved review accompanies the preservation rules. Review notes open beside the draft with actual checks; the fold choice is remembered per blog.
