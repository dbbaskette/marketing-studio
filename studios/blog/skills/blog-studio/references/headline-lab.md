# Headline and hook lab

Use [copywriting](modules/copywriting.md) only for the relevant passage. Generate
several useful alternatives from the actual article, audience, and voice.
Separate a title's topic promise from the opening's job of pulling the reader
into the argument. Explain meaningful differences briefly when selecting helps.
Avoid promises the article cannot fulfill, clickbait, and unsupported numbers.
A stronger hook need not be a question or statistic. Apply a chosen alternative
when authorized and save a recoverable revision; otherwise deliver options.
