# Research handoff

Harness Research owns investigation, synthesis and independent review. If it is
missing, stop and point to https://github.com/dbbaskette/harness-research-skill.
Intake is settled: preserve audience, purpose, scope and source permission. With
sites under Source scope, search them first; save pages only from under them
unless the public web is chosen too, and then use it only for gaps.

## Fresh synthesis research

1. Follow the installed helper's synthesis workflow in `.studio-research/`.
   Pass the actual learner audience and training task in free-text `purpose`,
   separately from scope and source mode. Prioritize concept sequence,
   misconceptions and grounded examples. Research supplies evidence, not a course.
2. Save the connected report and authored brief. A separate invocation reviews
   their exact digest, evidence, coverage and usefulness. Run `synthesis verify`
   and inspect the saved report. Drafts and rejected reviews block authoring.
3. Submit the case with `studio desk submit` as kind `research`, target `research`,
   with `{case}`: the absolute path of the complete, independently reviewed case, as
   the conversation reference describes. The desk records its checked bundle in the
   training and indexes its finding IDs for citation checks.
4. Show the person the research with its source limitations and gaps. Independent
   review is not their approval: it is Ready only when they mark it so, as the
   completion reference describes. Read `research/brief.md` and its evidence for the
   blueprint; retain conditions, counterfacts and unknowns.

Changed bundle bytes invalidate research and downstream inputs. Hash checks prove
saved integrity, not truth or live website currency. Refresh through the original
helper workflow, save a new dossier and obtain a new content-bound review.

A training the runner drives does this handoff itself. Report blocked network or
access honestly. Never describe incomplete research as current. Then read the
blueprint reference.

A saved record is not continued here: if `training.py status` or the page says the training is one, use Bring it in, Open on the desk or Start new work from this, as its page offers.

Native page: **Read saved section draft** shows read-only prose with its
section revision and source limits while the independent check is pending. It is
not checked evidence or a deliverable. **Save queued note** keeps a private concern;
it changes no scope/evidence and starts no work. After pausing or finishing, use
the existing research refresh impact preview to approve the actual change. Earlier
notes retain their original basis; checked output supersedes draft previews.
