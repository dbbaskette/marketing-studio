# Open saved unfinished work on the desk

Only migrate after the person requests it. Close older Training Studio instances first; the workspace will require Training Studio 4.7.0 or newer. Already configured older runtimes may ignore that requirement. Nothing installs or updates automatically.

Read `studio desk migration-preview --folder PATH`. It is read-only: explain retained materials, incomplete work, held dependencies, blockers and the finite existing-part editing scope. Never display its machine token. Cancel changes nothing. If requested, apply with `studio desk migrate --folder PATH --expected TOKEN` using the exact returned token. Changed records, content, sources, ownership or workers refuse; preview again.

Original session/run records, files, practice dependencies, History, research/source/guidance pins, Google links/baselines and genuine earlier decisions remain. Separate desk records carry current interpretation and conversation execution. A known earlier paused/stopped runner status becomes history only after the previewed change; failures, questions and incomplete materials are not accepted or completed. No work, Ready, approval or delivery follows migration. Later work beyond the listed retained targets requires an explicit checked `desk scope` change.

In a shared workspace, original records, declared shared retained inputs and the existing workspace record must match committed bytes and the staged index. Commit them through the existing workspace workflow first; migration does not commit those edits. Private sources/drawings remain local. Local-only workspaces do not need that shared-replay prerequisite.

A usable saved teaching plan and its declared inputs are required. A deck outline also needs its native plan and dependency checks. Early, unreadable, missing or unsupported work retains its original saved flow. Any worker marker prevents migration; saved-flow Stop/recovery may be needed first. Existing recovery may discard uncommitted work: never promise otherwise or remove markers yourself.

A failed paired write blocks ordinary reads, worker pickup and sharing. Retry the same explicit migrate command to verify committed publication or roll back uncommitted desk metadata and the workspace requirement; rollback asks for a new preview. Changed or unknown recovery records refuse. Preserve the records and report the refusal. Do not edit them by hand. Deleting all migration evidence externally cannot be detected from unchanged original records alone.

Finished work keeps its existing Open on the desk action. After migration, [completion](completion.md) supports only explicit person Ready or lossless local-deck return/set-aside; pending Google drafts retain their proof and cannot become Ready. Migration never delivers them.
