# Working incrementally in Marketing Studio

Start with a small, editable plan. Research the gaps in that plan, then build the
selected outputs. Keep useful results as they arrive. This usually gives the best
balance between responsiveness and depth for new training and presentations.

The [Create training flowcharts](design/create-training-task-flow.md) map the
current workflow and a proposed split into individual tasks. Separate boxes show
parallel work, dependencies and approval points; proposed changes are labeled.

1. **Propose scope quickly.** Training Studio and Presentation Studio use a bounded
   Quick-model scan to suggest sections, objectives, a slide story and research
   questions. This is provisional planning, not checked research. A timed-out scan
   still leaves an editable starting point. No deep research starts before approval.
2. **Edit locally and agree the outline.** Remove irrelevant sections, add the
   questions that matter, and select permitted sources. Save and reload without
   starting assistant work. A finished script can retain its direct presentation
   route; an existing task keeps its saved flow unless you explicitly adopt another.
3. **Research the approved scope.** Up to three workstreams can run at once. Keep
   completed section drafts when another section fails; retry the unfinished work.
   Open **Read saved section draft** to read completed prose while the final check
   is pending. The assembled research still needs an independent whole-report
   check; these drafts cannot be delivered as checked evidence.
   Section authors work concurrently, followed by a small final-brief assembly job.
   That job uses the saved combining time allowance, rather than a separate shorter
   timeout. If it times out, **Give it more time** applies to that job; completed
   section contributions remain available for retry. This does not raise the
   project spending limit or skip the independent check.
4. **Build selected materials.** For training, the approved outline and checked
   research feed teaching blueprints and the requested guides, briefs, slides or
   practice. Review those materials on the desk. Both deck studios let you correct
   slide text locally before approving it for design. Review and delivery remain
   separate decisions.
5. **Make small changes locally.** Wording and notes edits need no new evidence
   collection. When sources or scope change, use the affected-work preview, select
   the required sections, and refresh only their dependent work. Confirmation checks
   current source bytes even when a resting preview was cached briefly.
6. **Check the final result.** Compatible unchanged checks may be reused, but source,
   brand, font, renderer, order, neighbor and review-question changes invalidate
   dependent evidence. Final consistency and approval bind the exact delivered revision.

Use research-first when the audience or central claim depends on facts you do not
have. Keep that first pass focused on the decision needed to choose an outline;
then return to the same incremental process. Exhaustive research before agreeing
scope can spend time on material the eventual training or deck will never use.

Long model work should usually happen once **per accepted unit of scope**. Changed
inputs, failed work and an independent check can legitimately require more calls.
The Activity log distinguishes time to a first saved result, assistant activity,
checking and waiting for a person; unrecorded boundaries stay unknown.

Marketing Studio's home shows Unfinished drafts plus bounded summaries of saved
Needs you, In progress and Recent work. Creation-form summaries contain only the
title, step and saved time in private metadata; source excerpts and upload bytes
are not read for this overview. Project summaries use each item's ignored work folder.
These describe last-saved state, not live process health. Resume
opens the existing native work without approving its pending decision. Missing
workspaces and limited lists are stated explicitly.

## Pause without losing your place

Unfinished forms in Blog Studio, Presentation Studio, Training Studio and
Infographic Studio save privately. **Save and close** returns to Home, where
**Resume draft** identifies the saved title and step. **Start new** explicitly
handles the single unfinished-form slot. **Discard draft** asks before removing
that form and keeps existing projects and captured sources. Wait for the saved
status before closing the page. A save error or conflicting edit needs attention
before reloading. Forms saved before draft discovery was installed remain
resumable natively; their next save adds the shared-home summary.

In either deck studio, review one slide at a time using the slide picker or
**Previous slide** / **Next slide**. Open speaker notes when needed. Corrections,
feedback and review position persist across reloads. **Save corrections** starts
no assistant, research or design job. **Discard corrections** returns to the current
outline; approval covers the whole slide outline, not only the visible slide.
In Training Studio, the complete review stays in **Deck**, leaving other material
tabs reachable.

If another tab saved newer slide corrections, stale writes and approval stop.
**Copy all local corrections** preserves the complete local correction set in one
copy. **Compare latest corrections** identifies differing slides and feedback;
**Use latest corrections** asks before loading that version. The local recovery
copy remains on the page for manual reconciliation. Copy it before closing or
reloading: unsaved conflict recovery is held in memory. Nothing merges silently or
starts a design job. Another remote edit can require another comparison.

If the underlying outline changed, earlier corrections remain readable but cannot
approve the new version. Copy the corrections you need, discard the earlier draft,
then review and correct the current outline. Use **Ask for changes** when you want
an assistant revision instead of a local wording correction.

Infographic Studio retains a failed build's completed stages and offers the next
local retry. A failed preview retries the preview without composing again or
requesting another image. Changed page revisions require a fresh action. Uncertain
image requests retain their separate recovery process. After editing a failed
preview, Home distinguishes **create a preview** (none exists) from **update the
preview** (an earlier one exists). A failure for an older page revision cannot
pretend to be the current action.

## Read research before it is finished

New outline-first Training Studio runs propose at most four essential sections;
you can add scope explicitly during outline review. Research uses a focused first pass: at most eight
essential facts and five cited sources per approved area, with section prose
bounded to 500 words. The outline should preserve requests for minimal basics;
advanced detail stays optional rather than becoming another required investigation.
Within the same budget and permitted sources, investigators also look for a useful
tutorial, lab or worked example. Teaching structure and examples are attributed;
technical claims still need authoritative evidence. Saved runs keep their existing
policy. These bounds control work size, not provider response time.

**Read working draft** opens the saved research sections together as soon as one
is available. **Download working draft (.md)** saves that same partial draft,
including revision identifiers, sources and unfinished-section notices. It is
unreviewed research prose, not a completed learner guide or a ready material.
Final brief assembly is identified separately from section writing.

If the independent check leaves required sources unread, it stops with the checked
source count. Unread does not mean incorrect: the studio keeps the evidence and
prose instead of automatically reinvestigating or rewriting them. **Retry independent
check** reruns checking after its allowance, time or source-access issue is resolved.
The current single-reviewer protocol does not reuse partial semantic attestations.
Genuine content failures from a completed check still request affected corrections.
Obvious placeholder prose is rejected before independent review.

Choose **Read accepted findings — unreviewed** to see completed investigator
statements while slower peers continue. Sources, context and limitations stay
visible; the preview is incomplete, read-only and bounded. Retry replaces only the
affected attempt's view; cancellation and invalidation remove current evidence.
No additional model call is made for this preview, and it is not final evidence.

Expand the kept section drafts and choose **Read saved section draft**. Each preview
identifies its sources and revision and says that the independent check is pending.
It is read-only and may be shortened. Reading it starts no additional model work.

Use **Save queued note** to keep a concern for the next research change. The note
does not interrupt an investigator or alter in-flight evidence. When you choose to
change research, copy the concern into the explicit refresh and review its affected
scope before approving it. Typing after a save immediately marks the note unsaved,
including after returning to the review. Earlier-revision notes are marked for review. When the
checked report arrives, it supersedes these drafts; earlier drafts and notes remain
available for reference.

## Choose effort and recognize progress

The [Quick scan controls](shared-setup.md#quick-scan-speed) show the effective model
and reasoning. A lighter provisional scan can help you agree scope earlier; it
does not replace deep research or final checking. The 60-second first-outline
target is distinct from the scan's hard limits and is not a provider speed promise.

Idle Training Studio and Presentation Studio desks check for changes before fetching
the full content again. Large metadata scans continue across bounded requests.
A completed signal can be reused for up to 30 seconds while scanning continues;
errors or expiry fall back to full reads, and the next probe requests an
authoritative catch-up once the last full read is 60 seconds old. External changes may wait for a scan cycle or that catch-up.
This reduces content reads; it does not guarantee every probe is faster.
Active work still reports progress, and returning to the tab
or reconnecting catches up. A quiet desk does not mean another assistant pass is
needed. Use the recorded first-readable and checked-result times to distinguish
useful early progress from the final review boundary.

Slide preview publication still waits for the verified bundle. The provisional
preview experiment showed useful large-deck display gains, but its incomplete
dependency binding is not a production streaming contract. Final consistency and
exact-revision approval remain required.
