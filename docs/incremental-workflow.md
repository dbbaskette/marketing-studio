# Working incrementally in Marketing Studio

Start with a small, editable plan. Research the gaps in that plan, then build the
selected outputs. Keep useful results as they arrive. This usually gives the best
balance between responsiveness and depth for new training and presentations.

1. **Propose scope quickly.** Training Studio and Presentation Studio use a bounded
   Quick-model scan to suggest sections, objectives, a slide story and research
   questions. This is provisional planning, not checked research. A timed-out scan
   still leaves an editable starting point. No deep research starts before approval.
2. **Edit locally and agree the outline.** Remove irrelevant sections, add the
   questions that matter, and select permitted sources. Save and reload without
   starting assistant work. A finished script can retain its direct presentation
   route; an existing task keeps its saved flow unless you explicitly adopt another.
3. **Research the approved scope.** Up to three workstreams can run at once. Keep
   completed section drafts when another section fails; retry the unfinished work.
   The assembled research still needs an independent whole-report check. Retained
   drafts are useful progress, not independently approved final evidence.
4. **Build selected materials.** For training, the approved outline and checked
   research feed teaching blueprints and the requested guides, briefs, slides or
   practice. Review those materials on the desk. For presentations, approve slide
   text before design. Review and delivery remain separate decisions.
5. **Make small changes locally.** Wording and notes edits need no new evidence
   collection. When sources or scope change, use the affected-work preview, select
   the required sections, and refresh only their dependent work. Confirmation checks
   current source bytes even when a resting preview was cached briefly.
6. **Check the final result.** Compatible unchanged checks may be reused, but source,
   brand, font, renderer, order, neighbor and review-question changes invalidate
   dependent evidence. Final consistency and approval bind the exact delivered revision.

Use research-first when the audience or central claim depends on facts you do not
have. Keep that first pass focused on the decision needed to choose an outline;
then return to the same incremental process. Exhaustive research before agreeing
scope can spend time on material the eventual training or deck will never use.

Long model work should usually happen once **per accepted unit of scope**. Changed
inputs, failed work and an independent check can legitimately require more calls.
The Activity log distinguishes time to a first saved result, assistant activity,
checking and waiting for a person; unrecorded boundaries stay unknown.

Marketing Studio's home shows bounded summaries of saved Needs you, In progress
and Recent work from compact metadata under each item’s ignored work folder.
These describe last-saved state, not live process health. Resume
opens the existing native work without approving its pending decision. Missing
workspaces and limited lists are stated explicitly.

Infographic Studio retains a failed build's completed stages and offers the next
local retry. A failed preview retries the preview without composing again or
requesting another image. Changed page revisions require a fresh action. Uncertain
image requests retain their separate recovery process.
