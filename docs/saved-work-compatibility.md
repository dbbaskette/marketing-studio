# Saved work compatibility

New work starts on the desk. Conversation starts are focused and start no worker: ask for an outline, interview, research, passage or other supported scope, inspect the checked context, and save the requested changes. Expanding scope and handing work to the runner are explicit actions. Only a person approves; automatic continuation is recorded as continuation. Unfinished work remains Draft, and a delivered revision is distinct from Ready. Marketing Studio's Can open describes local launch prerequisites, not approval of a deck or training.

To migrate eligible saved work, explicitly request a preview. Blog Studio uses `desk migration-preview --article ID` and `desk migrate --article ID --expected TOKEN`; Presentation Studio and Training Studio use `desk migration-preview --folder PATH` and `desk migrate --folder PATH --expected TOKEN`. Use the token returned by the preview; cancellation changes nothing. Explain blockers and consequences before migration. Changed inputs or dependencies require another preview. The supported migration keeps original bytes, source and guidance pins, baselines and approvals, with a separate effective desk interpretation; it does not invent approval or delivery. See the product migration references for eligibility and commands.

## Work the earlier step-by-step flows saved

Since Marketing Studio 4.20.0 nothing continues work that Blog Studio, Presentation Studio or Training Studio saved in the earlier step-by-step flow (a saved record with no `flow: desk`). Continue, Try again, Answer, gate decisions and the earlier run and gate screens are gone for it. Each such piece of work opens as a saved record, which says what the work is and lists its saved files to read in place. It is in one of four states, and what the work holds decides first: work with nothing made yet is read-only whatever its workspace is doing.

- **Opens on the desk:** the work has a plan, outline or draft. **Open on the desk** is the explicit migration: a preview, then Apply with the preview's token. A saved failure, worker question, pause or stop is kept in History; the unfinished step stays unfinished; nothing is retried, answered, approved, marked Ready or delivered.
- **Finished:** delivered or finished work keeps what finished work could do before, including its own finished **Open on the desk**.
- **Waiting:** an opening on the desk that was interrupted (its one action is **Finish opening on the desk**, the migration's own recovery), a step that is really running, another computer driving the work, or something else in the workspace in progress. The record says what it waits for and what clears it, and offers new work from it meanwhile where nothing on the page clears the wait.
- **Read-only:** nothing was made yet (it stopped before a plan or outline, including a failure or question there), or a record or dependency it needs cannot be read or understood. Its files are never changed. Its one action is **Start new work from this**, or **Start a new …** where its start form cannot be read. Research already done on it is not carried into the new work, which starts from its start form and material and researches again.

Lists say only what the small saved records prove: Presentation Studio lists such work under **Saved by the earlier flow**, Training Studio under **Saved earlier**, and Blog Studio as **Saved by the earlier flow**, **Finished in the earlier flow** or that its records cannot be read. The state is said on the opened record, which checks the work when it opens.

Only these may change a saved-flow folder, the same list in all three studios: the explicit migration (its preview, Apply, and finishing an interrupted opening); **Take over**, the team's existing action, only where the work would open on the desk once taken, and refused on a read-only record; **Delete**, the person's own confirmed act; start-up recovery of an interrupted step, which does what it did before but never resumes or starts work, and which also removes leftover lock files so the workspace check does not stay busy; and, for finished work, what finished work could do before. **Hand over** is never offered on a saved record. Each studio enforces the list with one guard that refuses a saved record by default, with an explicit allow-list, pinned by a test that walks every changing route and command: Presentation Studio `studios/presentation/runner/saved_gate.py` (`tests/test_saved_gate.py`), Blog Studio `studios/blog/skills/blog-studio/scripts/saved_gate.py` (`tests/test_saved_gate.py`), and Training Studio `SAVED_ALLOWED` with `RunnerPage.saved_gate` in `studios/training/runner/page.py`, the desk command guard in `runner/studio.py` and the helper's `skills/training-studio/scripts/write_guard.py` (`tests/test_saved_guard.py`).

- **Blog Studio:** opens on the desk: a saved outline, draft, review or handoff, an approved outline before drafting, ideas or an outline-only run, an author's outline, a supplied manuscript's review or revision, a run continued without research, a run saved with a research execution record, and a paused, stopped or failed run. Outline notes the desk cannot read as sections are kept whole and shown on the desk. Finished: a finished blog keeps its Done card with its finished Open on the desk, publishing, downloads, the Google Doc, **Mark as published**, repurposing and the manuscript editor (Polish or edit the draft), and in a team **Record in team repository**. Waiting: an interrupted opening, a step running in this Blog Studio (Pause and Stop act only on it), another computer or an unreadable identity (Take), a tool task waiting for the person (it opens the task), and a workspace or computer condition it names, such as an older Team Hub. Read-only: angles, research questions, findings, claims maps and a brief-only start (nothing made), and records that cannot be read or understood; new work from it takes its title, point and audience, and nothing it researched or made. Start-up marks a step a stopped process left running on a saved run as stopped, in the runner's own record only (`Engine.settle_interrupted`).
- **Presentation Studio:** opens on the desk: a deck with a drafted outline (or, at Selected changes, its inventory), including one paused once its step ended, stopped, failed, or with a worker's question unanswered after its outline; record schemas 1 to 4, a schema 4 deck (parallel research) once its research is done and its outline drafted, with its research kept byte for byte. Finished: a deck delivered the earlier way keeps its finished Open on the desk, its delivery to read and download, and Publish. Waiting: an interrupted opening, a step running in this Presentation Studio, another computer running the deck (Take it over), shared files not yet committed, or something else in the workspace. Read-only: a deck stopped before its outline (a research plan or research stop, a document or an existing deck before classification, a question before the outline), a missing or changed source, research bundle or original deck, and a record the runner cannot read. Start-up recovers a stopped step in a saved deck this computer runs as it did before, without continuing it, and removes the step markers and lock files of a saved deck, or of any deck whose record cannot be read.
- **Training Studio:** opens on the desk: a stop at the blueprint or a later material gate, a waiting deck outline, look or deck, a saved pause or stop, and a failure or worker question once a teaching plan exists. Finished: a finished training keeps its finished Open on the desk and Publish. Waiting: an interrupted opening, a step running now, another computer running it (Take it over), shared originals not yet committed, or something else in progress in the workspace. Read-only: a stop before the teaching plan (the research plan or research, or a failure or question there), including work stopped inside the earlier flow's question-by-question research queue, and an unknown halt or update chain, a deck outline whose plan is missing or that cannot be read, a missing dependency, and run or session records that cannot be read or understood. Start-up recovery does for a saved training what it did before and starts nothing; a record that cannot be read at all loses only its leftover marker. A conversation training kept outside the workspace is brought in first.

Every saved state is pinned by a test that loads the work stored in its original format under each studio's `tests/fixtures/saved/`: `studios/blog/tests/test_saved_states.py`, `studios/presentation/tests/test_gated_saved_states.py` and `test_saved_records.py`, and `studios/training/tests/test_saved_states.py`. The stored bytes are saved work as the earlier flow wrote it; the capture that made them went with that flow, and `test_saved_fixtures.py` in each studio checks the stored trees and how a test loads them.

## Saved paths into a removed version

An update keeps one version of each studio and companion. A path saved earlier that names a
`versions/<id>` folder (a brand skill chosen in Settings, a job's slides or brand folder, a
companion task pin's `runtime`, slide guidance kept with a deck) is said through the `current`
link beside it when it is read, and nothing is rewritten. Research identities an older
Marketing Studio kept with a helper's digest (a case's name, section and final keys, the
unchanged-save context, the global research identity) are recognised as the same research
after an update and keyed anew as they are met; a run's record keeps what it held before, once,
as `research_execution.earlier`. A brand contract an older Harness Brand exported still holds,
with a notice to export it again.

## The check for running steps

Before sharing, starting or changing work in a conversation, or previewing a migration, the studios check the whole repository for a step still in progress (`core/studio_core/sync_guard.py`). The check recognises every run record Presentation Studio writes (schemas 1 to 6) and every run record Training Studio writes (schemas 1 to 8, from the runner and from conversation starts), so a workspace holding a desk deck started with research shares, starts and changes work and previews migrations as any other. Recognising a record does not make it eligible to migrate. A record the check cannot understand still stops these actions for the whole workspace, and its refusal now names the folder, relative to the repository, and why.

## Retained paths and exits

- **Blog Studio's manuscript editor check:** a current feature, unchanged. An existing registered non-desk manuscript with no run can create the review or check run internally, and the runs the editor's check makes (their record carries `editor_check`) are the only saved runs that still go on, from Edit manuscript; the runner, `submit`, `job` and the research stages stay for them. The editor refuses desk records and an unfinished saved run it did not make; a finished one keeps the editor. Migration requires a saved run and does not replace this check. `blog_runner.create` still accepts its other modes internally, and some branches for them remain in the code, unreachable by any person; a follow-up issue retires them.
- **Training Studio's conversation-guided helper commands:** `training.py` save, approve and the rest, `desk.py` `decide` and `gate`, and the gate screen they use, stay. No such training can be started any more; a follow-up issue retires them. `Engine.decided`, which the research update of an opened training uses, and the saved-record readers (`_policy_outline`, `_review_view`, `review_beads`) stay too.
- **Start-up recovery, Take over and Delete:** start-up recovery of an interrupted step, the team's Take over and the person's confirmed Delete keep working for saved work as described above.
- **Readers of older formats:** saved review levels, decisions, schemas and records stay readable, so the migration, the finished Open on the desk and `studio saved-work` keep reading them. Training Studio review layout 1 (a missing `review_layout` reads as layout 1) is read this way: such a training opens on the desk with its record unchanged, or, finished, keeps its finished Open on the desk; nothing continues it. The research question queue's records are read where work begun on it is opened or reported; no studio continues unfinished queue work, and work stopped inside it is read-only.
- **Presentation Studio cover sample:** saved pinned brand contracts and local cover state, including versionless and pre-looks brands, can require it. Retain saved continuation until those contracts and states have a supported replacement.
- **Older Harness Slides capability fallback:** retain uncertain probing until an explicit minimum and capability guard refuses unsupported tools before mutation and saved-format regressions prove continuation. Runtime age alone is insufficient.

A `saved-work` report examines only the declared local roots and candidates. Missing, unreadable, unsupported and excluded candidates can make a result unknown; it cannot certify other computers, unmounted workspaces, clones or recovery history. An empty local scan is not a retirement certificate. The report does not repair or migrate work.
