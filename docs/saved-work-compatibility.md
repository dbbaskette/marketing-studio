# Saved work compatibility

New work starts on the desk. Conversation starts are focused and start no worker: ask for an outline, interview, research, passage or other supported scope, inspect the checked context, and save the requested changes. Expanding scope and handing work to the runner are explicit actions. Only a person approves; automatic continuation is recorded as continuation. Unfinished work remains Draft, and a delivered revision is distinct from Ready. Marketing Studio's Ready to open describes local launch prerequisites, not approval of a deck or training.

To migrate eligible saved work, explicitly request a preview. Blog Studio uses `desk migration-preview --article ID` and `desk migrate --article ID --expected TOKEN`; Presentation Studio and Training Studio use `desk migration-preview --folder PATH` and `desk migrate --folder PATH --expected TOKEN`. Use the token returned by the preview; cancellation changes nothing. Explain blockers and consequences before migration. Changed inputs or dependencies require another preview. The supported migration keeps original bytes, source and guidance pins, baselines and approvals, with a separate effective desk interpretation; it does not invent approval or delivery. See the product migration references for eligibility and commands.

## Retained paths and exits

- **Gated screens and pipelines:** absent flow denotes older saved work. Continue supported runs or explicitly migrate eligible ones. Early work without a usable manuscript or outline, missing dependencies and unknown or unreadable recovery retain their path. Retire only after every supported saved state has proven lossless continuation or migration, with regression coverage.
- **Research question queue:** saved records without a research contract can continue unfinished queue work; schema-1 readers also support pins, index and readiness. Retire its writer only after a proven replacement for unfinished queues and regression fixtures; retain readers while saved formats need them.
- **Training Studio review layout 1:** missing `review_layout` selects layout 1, including its ordering and approval signatures. Retain its reader and continuation until compatible saved-format reading and continuation preserve those semantics.
- **Presentation Studio cover sample:** saved pinned brand contracts and local cover state, including versionless and pre-looks brands, can require it. Retain saved continuation until those contracts and states have a supported replacement.
- **Older Harness Slides capability fallback:** retain uncertain probing until an explicit minimum and capability guard refuses unsupported tools before mutation and saved-format regressions prove continuation. Runtime age alone is insufficient.

A `saved-work` report examines only the declared local roots and candidates. Missing, unreadable, unsupported and excluded candidates can make a result unknown; it cannot certify other computers, unmounted workspaces, clones or recovery history. An empty local scan is not a retirement certificate. The report does not repair or migrate work.
